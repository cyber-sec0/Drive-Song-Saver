## 🚀 The Perfect Companion for Spotify, YouTube Music, and More!
Never lose track of that amazing new song you discovered while driving. Whether you are listening to a new playlist or a random algorithm recommendation on your favorite streaming platform, Drive 🎵💾 captures it instantly. 

This app acts as your temporary staging ground, allowing you to quickly store new and amazing songs that you're listening to safely on the road so you can manually review, share, or add them to your favorite music platforms later when you are safely parked.

## Core Features

### One-Click Overlay Logging

A persistent, lightweight floating button draws over other applications (such as Google Maps) to save the currently playing track instantly. The overlay retains its exact position across device restarts and app launches.

### Local Media Logs View

The primary launch screen aggregates all saved tracks into a high-contrast dark theme list. Missing album art covers automatically fall back to clean system placeholders, while captured artwork stretches dynamically to fit the interface cleanly.

### Advanced Gesture Workflows

* **Hold to Copy:** Long-pressing any logged entry copies the full text layout directly to the Android clipboard.
* **Swipe to Highlight:** Swiping an item from left to right applies a light sky-blue background highlight to flag specific tracks. Swiping again toggles the highlight off.
* **Swipe to Delete:** Swiping right to left removes the specific log from the local Room database, immediately prompting an on-screen snackbar with an instant **UNDO** recovery option.

### Privacy & Performance Safeties

The app utilizes standard system notification listeners and media session managers to capture the available song metadata without requiring invasive file storage permissions. Extracted album art is dynamically downsampled to 128x128px to maintain a crash-free, zero-latency background memory footprint. Header controls on the logs view allow for icon-only global clearing and clean text-formatted multi-line sharing directly to external messaging apps like WhatsApp.
